Profile

My name is Marc Sau Batlle, a Unity Developer from Spain with more than 6 years of experience in the videogame industry in both Unity and C#. I have worked both solo and in medium sized teams, have experience managing and constructing growing systems with strict performance requirements while sticking to deadlines and I'm used to dealing with platform specific issues.

I'm a very outspoken person with a pro-active mindset who is not shy to ask questions till I get the full picture of what I'm working with, from debating with designers to helping out artists by making them tools to speed up their workflow, I am used to working in teams with varied opinions and points of view while maintaining good communication with all sides.

Overall I'm a friendly person that likes to discuss things and is eager to dip its toes into new territories.
